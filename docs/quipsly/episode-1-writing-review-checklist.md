## Fast read

Answer these first:

- Does the draft sound emotionally true to High Ground Odyssey?
- Does it respect Homer's voice, Charlie's voice, and the actual published Episode 1 context?
- Is it useful enough to become a real page, post, or supporting article after revision?
- Does anything feel like fake certainty, fake sourcing, or a claim that needs a receipt?
- Does the Tower packet make publication easier without implying anything is already published?

## Authorship and state check

Required labels before promotion:

- Authorship is visible: `agent-authored`, `human-authored`, or `mixed-authorship`.
- Review state is visible: `agent-first-pass`, `human-reviewed`, `needs-revision`, or `canon-approved`.
- Canon state is visible: not canon-approved until Charlie/Homer explicitly approve it.
- Publication state is visible: not published until a destination receipt exists.
- Source context is visible or uncertainty is marked where factual claims matter.

## Revision checklist

Before canon approval, decide whether to revise for:

- Voice: warmer, funnier, more grounded, less generic.
- Specificity: more Episode 1 details, fewer abstract claims.
- Source truth: quote or link exact source material if factual claims matter.
- Structure: clearer beginning, middle, end, and call to action.
- Tower fit: one version for HGO page, one for Patreon, one for social, one for YouTube support copy if needed.

## Approval outcomes

Pick one:

- `needs-agent-revision`: ask Codex/Quipsly to revise with specific notes.
- `needs-human-rewrite`: keep the structure but rewrite in human voice.
- `mixed-authorship-ready`: humans have revised enough that it can proceed as mixed-authorship.
- `canon-approved`: approved as part of High Ground Odyssey truth.
- `publication-ready`: ready for Tower scheduling/posting, pending destination-specific checks.

## Receipt rule

Do not mark any external publication complete until Tower captures at least one of:

- public URL,
- scheduled URL,
- provider receipt ID,
- platform-native confirmation copied into Quipsly.

Prepared copy is not publication. Published means there is a receipt.
