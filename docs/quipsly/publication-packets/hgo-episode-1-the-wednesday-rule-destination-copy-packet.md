### Quote post

### LinkedIn-style post

One of the most useful leadership skills is learning how to restart without
turning delay into a referendum on your character.

The Wednesday Rule is simple:

The week is not over because Monday went badly.

That applies to teams, creative work, operations, coaching, and almost every
project worth doing. Good systems should make re-entry easier. Bad systems make
you pay a shame tax before you can continue.

We are building Quipsly around that belief: capture the thread, preserve the
context, show the next honest action, and keep the work moving.
