# Episode 4 audio-first publication goal

## Objective

Make Episode 4 the best publishable episode Quipsly can produce, with the high-quality audio spine as the first gate and final episode/short exports as downstream branches. Charlie should be able to listen after grave shift and decide whether Episode 4 is ready for Spotify, Apple Podcasts, YouTube, and manual publication.

## Priority rule

Audio spine first. Final episodes and shorts inherit the spine only after the v006/v007 candidate passes human listening or a clearly scoped repair pass.

## Work loop

1. Strengthen machine quality checks for the Episode 4 audio spine: loudness, true peak, clipping, silence, speaker contribution, bleed/echo, noise floor, phase/mono safety, transcript coverage, sync, and focused A/B proof windows.
2. Keep human listening as the naturalness gate. Machine checks can prove safety and surface risks; they cannot certify cadence, humor, warmth, or whether the episode feels human.
3. If v006 passes, unlock branch inheritance and render the best Episode 4 long-form episode plus shorts from the approved spine.
4. If v006 fails, create a scoped v007 repair plan and proof windows before any full rerender.
5. Use the Episodes 1-6 rollout board to apply the same tooling to other episodes without blocking Episode 4.
6. When Episode 4 is gated on Charlie listening, improve Episodes 1-6 intake, source inventory, transcript/audio evidence, shorts readiness, and reusable cleanup profiles.
7. Never overwrite previous versions and never mutate original media.
8. Do not publish, upload, schedule, or claim receipts without explicit approval.

## Acceptance

- Episode 4 has a current-best audio spine with machine QC, listen launcher, notes front door, and honest approval state.
- Episode 4 has a clear pass/fail/focused-proof path after Charlie listens.
- Final episode and shorts branches remain locked until the audio spine is approved.
- Episodes 1-6 have an intake/readiness board for applying the same audio workflow.
- Every artifact is inspectable, reversible, versioned, and honest about what is ready.
