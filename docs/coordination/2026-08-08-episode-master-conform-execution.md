# Episode master conform execution boundary

Date: 2026-08-08

This checkpoint turns an approved full-program review into a durable, local-only 4K master-candidate job. It does not turn approval into publication.

## Frozen job evidence

`quipsly-episode-master-conform-job-v1` binds:

- the latest exact `APPROVED` program-review receipt;
- the approved branch revision and timeline/source/edit fingerprints;
- the complete approved program-render manifest, including every original source generation and output-clock chunk;
- the reviewed 720p output hash, generation, and byte count as approval provenance only;
- one executor node and storage scope;
- the `3840x2160`, 24 fps, H.264 CRF 17 medium, AAC 320 kbps/48 kHz profile; and
- a canonical SHA-256 manifest digest.

The 720p candidate is never an input to master rendering. The local worker opens the exact original locators embedded in the approved program manifest.

## Queue and execution

The server plans capacity without side effects. Queueing then repeats the latest-decision, current-branch, and exact review-result checks under a serializable transaction and advisory lock.

The Mac worker:

1. claims only jobs addressed to its node and storage scope;
2. validates the canonical manifest digest;
3. hashes and sizes every original before encoding;
4. renders generation-locked chunks and renews its durable lease after each chunk;
5. assembles the chunks with MP4 fast-start;
6. probes and completely decodes the 4K output;
7. hashes every original again before committing the result; and
8. emits an approval-bound result receipt.

Dedicated media roots may live on internal or external durable volumes. Filesystem root, home root, and the operating-system temporary root remain forbidden; all source and target paths must resolve inside the configured workspace.

## Registration and playback

Server registration independently verifies the output path, SHA-256, and byte count, then repeats the current-approval/current-branch check. A superseded decision retains the generated bytes but cannot become the current candidate.

A successful registration creates protected executor-local playback with the role `episode-master-candidate`. The browser labels it unapproved and unpublished. Master review, portable upload, delivery encoding, and publication remain later explicit receipts.
