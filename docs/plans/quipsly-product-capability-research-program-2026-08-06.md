# Quipsly product-capability research program

Date: 2026-08-06
Status: queued after the current recording and source-truth foundation

## Purpose

Research the largest credible improvements Quipsly can make to existing
capabilities and the highest-leverage new capabilities it can add. The output
must be a decision-ready product and architecture program, not a feature dump
or a competitor-cloning exercise.

## Research lenses

Every capability is evaluated through the same lenses:

- creator job and failure cost;
- current Quipsly workflow and evidence gaps;
- leading and adjacent product patterns;
- first-session comprehension and expert depth;
- collaboration, permissions, consent, provenance, and reversibility;
- offline, degraded-network, recovery, and long-running-work behavior;
- accessibility across phone, browser, and native editor;
- storage, inference, media-processing, and support cost;
- canonical data ownership and cross-surface continuity;
- production acceptance test using real work.

## Capability tracks

1. Capture and live collaboration: local masters, external devices, shared
   playback, clock evidence, recovery, and browser/iPhone/Mac combinations.
2. Audio mastery: trustworthy meters, diagnostics, repair proposals,
   loudness/noise/mouth-sound transparency, and reversible mastering.
3. Transcription and knowledge extraction: diarization, vocabulary, correction
   learning, citations, uncertainty, notes, decisions, goals, and tasks.
4. Automated editing: transcript, audio, camera, clip, and semantic evidence;
   explainable proposals; fast human review; and deterministic exports.
5. Writing and research: source capture, annotation, synthesis, manuscript
   structure, traceable claims, examples, and publication handoff.
6. Projects and collaboration: nests, tags, relationships, chat, ownership,
   notifications, calendars, goals, tasks, and reusable workflows.
7. Coaching and learning: engagements, session continuity, shared plans,
   private boundaries, curricula, exercises, assessment, and LMS delivery.
8. Publishing and growth: versioned assets, channel packages, social planning,
   hosting, analytics feedback, and rights/approval ledgers.

## Deliverables

- a capability map showing what is real, partial, misleading, or absent;
- a task-level competitive workflow study with primary-source evidence;
- a usability audit of current Quipsly surfaces using retained test users;
- opportunity briefs with UX storyboards and canonical ownership diagrams;
- a ranked portfolio separating foundation, obvious leap, differentiator, and
  speculative bet;
- cost and operational envelopes for media, storage, transcription, and AI;
- implementation slices with migration and rollback strategy; and
- an operated acceptance script for every promoted capability.

## Prioritization rule

Quipsly should first remove failures that can lose, misrepresent, or strand a
creator's work. Next come improvements that collapse a multi-tool workflow into
one calmer loop. Differentiators follow when the underlying evidence and
recovery boundaries are trustworthy. A large feature earns priority when it
improves the complete creator journey, not merely because a competitor has it.

## Initial research order

1. Audio transparency, mastery automation, and transcription accuracy.
2. Reliable multi-device recording and immediately editable synchronized
   sources.
3. Session-to-notes/tasks/goals continuity for coaching and production.
4. Explainable automated video editing with rapid review.
5. Unified research, writing, collaboration, and publishing workflows.

Each track ends with an explicit build, defer, combine, or reject decision and
the evidence supporting it.
