# Quipsly Private Beta: Welcome, Founders

We are beyond excited to announce that the **Quipsly Private Beta is officially live for our Patreon supporters!**

If you've been following our thesis on "Systems Anxiety," you know exactly why we built Quipsly. We believe that technology should empower your voice, not replace it. Quipslys are your enthusiastic research assistants, your librarians, and your media organizers—here to handle the citations, the lore bibles, and the chaotic administrative side of creative work so you have the freedom and clarity to do what you do best: write, dream, and create.

## How to Access the Beta

1. Go to [Quipsly.com](https://quipsly.com)
2. Click **"Sign in with Patreon"**
3. Once authenticated, you will be directed to our Beta Welcome Hub.

**Important Note on Account Reconciliation:** 
Access to the beta requires manual reconciliation with your Patreon tier. When you sign in, your account might enter a "pending" state. Please give our team a few hours to flip the switch for your account. You can return to Quipsly.com anytime to check your status.

## What You Can Do Today

We have been sprinting to build a concrete, usable product that proves our philosophy. In this beta, you can:

- **Create a Living Manuscript:** Draft your articles, scripts, or podcast show notes in a beautiful, unified workspace.
- **Import Media:** Bring in your raw video or audio clips. We'll help you log the best moments and build precision edit markers.
- **Consult your Quipsly:** Ask your Quipsly to pull verifiable quotes and exact citations to build rich research packets.
- **Prepare Publishing Packets:** Assemble everything you need for a smooth publishing session.

## Beta Expectations

This is a true beta. It is not a polished marketing demo. You are the very first people outside of our team to use Quipsly. 
- You will encounter bugs. 
- You will find rough edges in the UI.
- Some features (like full mobile recording sync) are still actively being polished.

But your data is safe. We do not use your private Nests to train generalized AI models. 

We want your raw, honest feedback. Let us know where you feel friction, what delights you, and what your Quipsly can do better.

Thank you for believing in a future where creators hold the pen. 

Let's build wisdom together.
